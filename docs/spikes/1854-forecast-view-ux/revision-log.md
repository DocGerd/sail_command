# #1854 design: revision log

The revision agent's record of how it disposed of the critique findings in `critique.json`.

Written before the claim audit on PR #1872; `../1854-forecast-view-ux.md` supersedes it where they differ.

Blocker/major findings

1. [blocker] "kein Seegang" reads as calm; noSea blames the hour. APPLIED, fixed together with #12 (§5 place readout, §7, §8). The words "kein Seegang"/"no sea state" are gone. Location and hour get separate keys: `conditions.place.seaNone` ("Seegang: keine Modelldaten in Hafennähe") and `conditions.sea.noHour` ("Keine Seegangsdaten für diese Stunde – Daten bis …"), following `route.waves.outOfRange` (dict.de.ts:861). Unit test over every harbour: AC-16, §14.
2. [blocker] Frozen clock on the tab. APPLIED (§4 Clock, §7 Ready/Stale/Refetch, §9 Announcements, AC-8). A 60 s tick runs only while Wetter is active and the page is visible, plus a recompute on `visibilitychange`. State comes from an interval effect, never `Date.now()` in render (RouteLayer.tsx ~:1182 lint note). The age text sits outside the polite region, which announces only transitions. The handoff reads `Date.now()` at press time. AC-8 adds the 13 h fake-clock case.
3. [major] Offline handoff plans on nothing / on the plan's grid. APPLIED-NARROWED (§11 Offline, §7, §0 Status, AC-6). Verified: `storedWindGrid` is passed only under an active next-passage seed (App.tsx ~:1404 `activeSeed && plan`), so "with a plan loaded it silently plans on the plan's older grid" holds only for that seed, not for any loaded plan. Passing the Wetter session grid to `run()` offline would widen the source spec's Offline-scope row (line 26), a design-level deviation. So this design takes the visible-note option (`conditions.useAsDeparture.offline`) and records the session-grid fallback as an open question for the maintainer. §11's "same grid" claim is scoped to online within the TTL. e2e asserts the note.
4. [major] Harbour taps silently edit the planner. APPLIED, fixed together with #13 (§2 step 4, §5 Popup, §6 Harbour taps, §8, AC-9/AC-10). On Wetter a harbour tap opens that harbour's forecast popup with [Als Start]/[Als Ziel] (≥44 px); a bare tap never mutates Start or Ziel. The Ziel line shows Ziel's conditions. The "wird Start, dann Ziel" hint is replaced by `conditions.start.none`.
5. [major] Three unlabelled clocks. APPLIED (§3 sticky hour row, §6 first row, §8 `conditions.planForecast`, AC-7, AC-18). On Wetter the route draws in the alt-rig style (dash [1, 1.5], 0.45); `ANNOTATION_LAYERS` and `sc-maneuver-labels` are hidden; the persisted alt-rig overlay is hidden too (without writing its toggle), so two dashed routes cannot coexist. The hour row is sticky, with the −12 px content-box note cited and the geometry pinned by AC-18 rather than a CSS value. The plan line says "abgerufen" because `fetchedAtMs` is the fetch time. No on-map time: `.route-layer-controls` is not rendered on Wetter (minor 17).
6. [major] Depth and sea-state ramps tint the same water. APPLIED, one decision with #18 (§6 Depth row, §3 item 6, §8, AC-17, §14 real browser). On Wetter while sea state is visible, DataLayers hides `sc-depth` and `sc-depth-hatch` through a `suppressDepth` term in its visibility effect (DataLayers.tsx ~:896) and does not render the depth Legende; persisted toggles are untouched. A visible card line with [Tiefen zeigen] sets a per-visit App flag that lifts it; leaving the tab resets it. STOPS colours cited from depthColor.ts ~:133–140.
7. [major] Disclaimer, barb key and arrow conventions hidden at the floor; "225°" reads as a heading; "Wetter" over-promises. APPLIED-NARROWED (§3 item 5, §8 `conditions.notice`, `conditions.place.wind`, `conditions.area`). An always-visible notice line states planning aid, wind and sea state only, and both arrow conventions. Directions read "aus SW (225°)" in the place and area lines. Conflict with #16 resolved: the strip keeps 3 rows, so its direction is in each column's accessible name (sr-only "Wind aus {cardinal}", §9), not visible text.
8. [major] Narrow height budget omits rows; status below the fold; AC-11 tests the easy state. APPLIED, fixed together with #16 as one anatomy (§3 Card anatomy and Tablet narrow, AC-11). Status chip at the top with alerts under it; actions above the strip; on narrow the Disclosure (area line, scrubber, day buttons, key) is closed by default, and « » day steppers in the hour row replace day buttons for compact navigation. AC-11 is restated at age ≥10 min (update button visible), chip and actions inside the viewport at scrollTop 0, with and without a toast and a banner. The new budget (609–641 px) is labelled as an estimate.
9. [major] Current-hour handoff disabled. APPLIED (§11 The button, §7 Current hour, §8 `conditions.useAsDeparture.now`, AC-6). "Jetzt als Abfahrt" sets `Date.now()` rounded up to the next 5 min, read at press time; the button is never disabled. Cited: PlannerPanel.tsx ~:772–778 calls min/max soft guidance computed at mount, and no past-departure validation was found in app/src (grep at 7a08b939). `conditions.useAsDeparture.past` removed.
10. [major] Summaries move under pan; "Ganzes Revier" cannot deliver its name. APPLIED, fixed together with #21 (§3 Fixed scope, §1 goal, §3 Tablet narrow, §10 RouteLayer, §8). The strip, sparkline and ribbon use the Start's place readout, else the whole Revier, with the scope in the strip label; only the area line follows the viewport and is labelled. "Ganzes Revier" is renamed "Revier zeigen", fits with the #1626 `measureChrome()` occluder padding and accepts a clamped partial fit; §1 and §3 state that at 820 the area is reached by panning.
11. [major] Tab overflow fix keyed on the viewport; wide panel unguarded. APPLIED, fixed together with #20 (§3 Tab strip, AC-12). Horizontal padding drops to 0.25rem everywhere (flex: 1 sets the width), and a container query on `.app-tabs` adds a font step below 22.5rem. The fallback is named (engines without container queries keep the padding cut; browser versions are not cited from memory); `.app-tabs` is already `position: relative; z-index: 3` (app.css ~:568), so layout containment adds no new stacking. AC-12 extended to 1024 default, 1180x820 at default and 320 px, DE and EN, bold selected tab.
12. [blocker] Start line has no sea state for most harbours. APPLIED (§5 Place readout, §3 mockups, AC-16). Re-measured with `harbour_wave.py` against the committed mask.bin and harbors.json at 7a08b939, using the critic's method: 134 of 322 lattice points are in-mask water; 28 of 46 harbours have a non-water nearest point (Flensburg among them). Rule: nearest in-mask water point among the place's own and eight adjacent lattice points, labelled with distance and compass point; 44 harbours get a model point, Flensburg and Aabenraa get `seaNone` (nearest water about 8.4 and 7.1 nm). Distances are in nautical miles, not the critic's km, per the domain rule. The Flensburg mockup line now shows `seaNone`. No neighbour shading, so §15's no-borrowing rule holds.
13. [major] Harbour tap rewrites routing inputs, AC-10 lets harbours beat the popup. APPLIED, fixed together with #4 (§5 Popup, §6, AC-9, AC-10). AC-9 now says a harbour tap opens that harbour's forecast and leaves Start and Ziel unchanged until a button is pressed. AC-10 is reworded: the harbour path and the water click converge on one popup owner (WaveLayer), and the existing guard (WaveLayer.tsx ~:239–256) still keeps the water popup off harbour hits. Other tabs unchanged.
14. [major] Focus pulled out of the tablist when data arrives. APPLIED (§9 Focus). Focus never moves on load; status goes through the polite region; programmatic focus only on user actions (handoff, pressed stepper/day/strip keeps focus).
15. [major] ConditionsPanel has no map instance. APPLIED (§10 diagram and Placement, PR B list). ConditionsPanel mounts inside MapView and portals into a Wetter `.app-panel` slot, as SeamarksInView does into `seamarksSlot` (App.tsx ~:1821, verified); the mask is a NavMask view over loadRoutingAssets()' cached buffer. Both are drawn in the diagram.
16. [major] Budget omits horizon line, update button, strip text height, toast. APPLIED, fixed together with #8 (§3 Card anatomy, Tablet narrow, AC-11). Actions moved directly under the place lines, above the strip; strip cut to 3 rows (time, "wind (gust)", Hs) with units in a leading label column; `conditions.horizon.end` folded into the status chip; the toast-aware cap `calc(55vh - var(--sc-toast-height, 0px))` (app.css ~:5361) cited; AC-11 measured with and without a toast and a banner.
17. [major] AC-3 positive control does not discriminate. APPLIED (AC-3, §14 e2e). The probe is an A/B of the same pixels at a settled camera (two byte-equal screenshots per state), toggling only sea-state visibility: the water pixel must change, the land pixel must not. Mutation check: drawing today's rectangles reds the land half.
18. [major] Depth/sea-state interplay answered only by stack order. APPLIED, one decision with #6 (§6, AC-17, §14 real browser). The explicit decision is suppression on Wetter while sea state is visible, with a visible line and a coastal-shallow browser check in both themes.
19. [major] Barbs and wave markers share anchor points. APPLIED-NARROWED (§5 Marker placement, AC-4, §14). Verified: one lattice for both (openMeteo.ts latLonParams), barbs anchored on lattice indices (routeGeoJson.ts ~:306–314), barb icon centred with the station 12 px downwind and feathers at the upwind tip (windBarbs.ts ~:13–16, default icon-anchor). While barbs are visible, a marker sits at the quarter-cell point of its own cell on the downwind side, farther from the wind axis; markers moving between quadrants while scrubbing is stated as an accepted cost. It needs the hour's wind, so it lands in PR B and also changes plan mode; AC-4's placement clause is scoped to PR B. Pinned by a unit test of the rule and the daylight browser check; stated as "no marker on a shaft or feathers", not a general collision-free claim.
20. [major] Five-tab overflow on the wide panel. APPLIED, fixed together with #11.
21. [major] Whole area cannot be seen at the floor; "Ganzes Revier" promises it. APPLIED, fixed together with #10. Compact narrow card state is the #8/#16 anatomy.

Minor findings

The slider moved into the Disclosure, closed by default on narrow; « » day steppers in the sticky hour row cover day navigation. `touch-action: pan-y` was not added: it is unverified that it stops a vertical swipe from setting a range input.
