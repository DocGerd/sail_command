The depth-navigability hatch now picks its stripe width per zoom band instead of using one fixed cell-space pattern at every zoom, so the stripes stay legible instead of washing out to a faint haze when zoomed out. Measured in a real browser, the on-screen stripe at the app's starting zoom goes from about 1 px to about 8 px, and the wide bands at harbour-approach zoom are halved.
Which water the hatch marks is unchanged — this affects only how that marking is drawn.
