- The nautical scale bar now renders on short landscape phones (e.g.
  740x360) instead of being suppressed on every tab: the top-left map
  controls (water-depth and seamark toggles, compass) lay out as a compact
  row instead of a stacked column there, freeing the vertical room the
  scale bar needs. The scale bar can still be suppressed while an app
  banner (e.g. an available-update notice) is showing, since a banner
  pushes this control cluster down further (#441) (#231).
