Shallow-water relaxation is now confined to harbour approaches. When a route
cannot be planned at your safety depth, SailCommand may still relax the depth
gate to reach the destination (#53) — but that relaxation now applies only
within 1 nm of a waypoint you actually chose, instead of along the whole
passage. A shallow pinch far from every waypoint is reported honestly as
unreachable rather than routed through. Each waypoint's approach also gets
its own gate, so an approach that needs no relaxation is tightened back to
within 0.1 m of your safety depth instead of inheriting the shallowest gate
the passage needed anywhere.
