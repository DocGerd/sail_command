- New "Wetter" tab, second after Planen: wind and sea state for the whole sailing area at an hour you choose, without a planned route. Step by hour or day or drag the timeline; "Als Abfahrt übernehmen" hands that hour to the planner as its departure. The forecast is fetched only when the tab is opened and held for the session; offline the tab shows the held forecast with its age, and planning still needs a connection. While sea state is shown there, depth shading is hidden until "Tiefen zeigen". With barbs on, sea-state arrows now sit beside the barbs instead of under them (#1854).
