- Adding, removing, reordering, or dragging a waypoint no longer recalculates
  the route in the background: previously, every planner control locked up
  for the whole recalculation with no stated reason. A waypoint edit is now a
  plain, instant change to the plan — press "Plan route" to apply it, exactly
  like any other trip change — and the app shows a clear "not yet applied"
  indicator, both in the planner panel and on the map, until you do (#571).
