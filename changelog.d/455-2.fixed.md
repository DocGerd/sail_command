The About dialog now discloses the depth mask's remaining uncertainty: depth
values blend two readings of the same EMODnet bathymetry data that can
disagree by up to 0.9 m. At the default 3.0 m safety depth the more cautious
reading of every routable cell still meets the boat's 2.1 m draft; lowering
the safety depth narrows that margin, down to 1.3 m at the settings panel's
2.2 m minimum (#455).
