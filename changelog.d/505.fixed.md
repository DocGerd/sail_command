The depth profile's "min." figure could understate how shallow a route
actually gets: it was sampled uniformly in time (up to 240 points across the
whole trip), so a leg shorter than the sample interval could fall between two
ticks and be skipped entirely — on one observed route the profile read 2.9 m
while the shallow-water banner correctly reported 2.3 m for the same route.
The headline figure is now computed by walking every leg's actual charted
cells, the same exhaustive method the banner uses, so a short leg can no
longer be missed (#505).
