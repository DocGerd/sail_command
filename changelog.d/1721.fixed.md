Planning a boat to or from a harbour that the app's depth grid cannot connect to open water at the chosen safety depth now plans from or to a handover point up to 1 nm off the harbour, in water the grid connects, and shows the harbour approach as a striped line marked as not computed and not depth-checked; distance and times cover the computed route only. When no such point exists, the no-route message names the harbour, boat, draft and the safety-depth range checked, and the harbour picker shows that range before planning (#1721).
