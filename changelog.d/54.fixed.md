A saved plan the app could not read no longer disappears from the Routes
list. Such a plan was previously skipped, so from where a sailor sits it was
indistinguishable from having been deleted, while its data was still on the
device. It is now listed with its name and creation date, saying either that
a newer version of the app wrote it — in which case that version can still
read it — or that the saved record is incomplete, and it is never deleted
automatically (#54).
