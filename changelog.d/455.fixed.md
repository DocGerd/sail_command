The depth mask no longer lets a smoothed depth reading run far ahead of the
most conservative reading of the source bathymetry. At the default 3.0 m
safety depth, every cell the router may now plan through has a conservative
depth of at least the boat's 2.1 m draft; previously 924 cells were routable
at that setting despite reading below draft. Lowering the safety depth below
the default lowers that floor by the same amount. Some planned routes change
as a result — a few become shorter, where the mask had been reading water
shallower than the source supports (#455).
