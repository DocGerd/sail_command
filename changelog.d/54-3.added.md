Two Flensburg fleet boats gain polar tables in the shipped data: the Salona 44 (SPEEDY GO!) and the Elan Impression 444 (PIRANJA). Both are **estimated**, not measured — no ORC/IRC certificate or published VPP was obtainable for either hull, so each table is the Salona 45’s certificate-anchored jib table scaled by a single sail-area/displacement ratio, and each inherits the Salona 45’s pointing angles rather than deriving its own. Speeds are typically within a few percent, up to about ten percent in individual conditions, and the error matters most in light air, where it can flip a leg between sail and motor. Every table says all of this in its own source note.
