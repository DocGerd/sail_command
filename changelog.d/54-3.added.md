Two Flensburg fleet boats join the boat catalogue: the Salona 44 (SPEEDY GO!) and the Elan Impression 444 (PIRANJA). Their polar tables are **estimated**, not measured — no ORC/IRC certificate or published VPP was obtainable for either hull, so each table is the Salona 45's certificate-anchored jib table scaled by a single sail-area/displacement scalar. Speeds are typically within a few percent, up to about ten percent in individual conditions, and the error is largest in light air, where it can flip a leg between sail and motor. Because the difference between each boat's two sails comes from the Salona 45's overlay ramp rather than from the hull, the sail comparison is withheld for these boats rather than presented as a finding. Each entry also states which keel its draft assumes, since that figure comes from the operator's published tech sheet and not from the individual hull's papers.
